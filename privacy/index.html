1. What we collect and why

Your account

  • Email address and sign-in method (email and password, Sign in with Apple, or Google), to create and secure your account and send account emails. With Apple's Hide My Email we only get Apple's relay address. Your email is never shown to other users or used as your username.
  • Your name from Google or Apple: if you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and profile picture with our sign-in provider; if you sign in with Apple and choose to share your name, Apple shares it. If you haven't set a display name, we use that name as your display name, which other users can see; you can change or clear it in Edit Profile. We don't use the Google profile picture.
  • Username, optional display name, bio and profile photo, which other users see. If you haven't set a username or name, other users see a placeholder like "angler1234".
  • Sign-in records kept by our authentication provider (Supabase): the time of each sign-in and, for each active session, the IP address and device type, to keep you signed in and protect your account.
  • Age confirmation: to create an account you confirm you're at least 13. We don't ask for or store your date of birth, and we don't store the checkbox.

Location (only if you allow it)

  • Your current location, while you use the app, to find nearby waters, scores and stockings. Before it leaves your phone for these lookups it is rounded to about 0.7 mile, and it is not saved to your account. Our own server logs record only which page was requested, never your location or IP address. Our hosting provider (Render) keeps its own request logs for 14 days, and those can include your IP address.
  • Catch location (optional, off by default): if you turn it on for a catch, its exact spot is saved with that catch. Only you can see it, and it's never included when you share a catch to the feed. You can remove it by editing the catch.
  • Stocking alert area (Premium, optional): the center and radius you pick on the map, rounded to about 0.7 mile before it's saved. Turning the alert off deletes it.
  • Map telemetry (Mapbox): while the map is open, Mapbox's software may collect anonymized location data. You can opt out with the map's (i) button - see "Maps" in section 3.
  • Saved pins you drop on the map are private to you until you delete them.

What you create

  • Posts, comments, chat messages and photos you share in the community.
  • Angler reports: water clarity, crowding, water level, fish activity and an optional note for a stream. Shown to other users for 48 hours and deleted after 30 days.
  • Your catch log (species, size, fly, notes, photos, the conditions at the time, and an optional location). Private to you unless you choose "Post to feed", which shares species, fly, size, notes and your first photo, but not the location or stream name.
  • Saved streams, follows, state follows, likes, blocks and reports, so those features work.
  • Feedback you send from the app (subject, message, app version and platform).

Photos are re-saved as new JPEG images before upload, which removes embedded photo metadata such as GPS tags.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications we store your device's push token to deliver them. You only get the alerts you turn on: stocking alerts for a saved stream (one stream free, all with Premium), stocking alerts for your chosen area (Premium), and announcements for states you follow. We keep a record of what we sent so you get at most one alert a day.

Subscription

Whether you have Premium and when it renews. Apple handles payment; we never see your card details.

Map usage (collected by Mapbox)

Our maps are provided by Mapbox. Mapbox's software collects anonymized usage and location data, along with your device model, operating system, and an app installation identifier, to operate and improve its maps. You can opt out at any time by tapping the (i) button on the map and choosing 'Don't Participate.' Mapbox does not use advertising identifiers.

On your phone

The app keeps some information on your phone so it works quickly and offline: your sign-in session, your settings, map data, and copies of what you've recently viewed. It stays on your phone and is removed when you delete the app.

Crash reports

If the app crashes or hits an error, a report goes to Sentry: the error, app version, device model and OS version, and the last few network requests the app made, with IDs, emails and tokens removed. We don't attach your account, email or name, and screenshots, screen recording, usage sessions and performance tracing are all off. IP address storage is turned off in Sentry. Crash reports from the phone's system layer include a random identifier for the app installation (not connected to your account) and device details such as model and OS version. Crash reports are kept for 30 days.

5. Reports, blocks and moderation

You can report posts, comments, chat messages, angler reports and users, and block anyone. You stop seeing reported content right away, and content reported by several people is hidden from everyone until we review it. We aim to review reports within 24 hours.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your account and content: until you delete them or your account.
  • Things you delete (posts, comments, chat messages): their text and photos are erased right away. If someone has reported the item, we keep it until we've reviewed the report, then erase it; a nightly check erases anything left over within 7 days. An empty placeholder stays so replies still make sense.
  • Angler reports: deleted 30 days after they're posted.
  • Feedback: kept up to 12 months, and deleted when you delete your account.
  • Feedback and report emails in our support inbox: deleted by hand once they're more than 12 months old. We review the inbox every three months, so a copy can remain for up to about 15 months.
  • Reports and moderation records: kept 12 months after they're filed; open reports are kept until reviewed. [ATTORNEY: confirm retention statement]
  • Push delivery receipts: 7 days. Push tokens: until the device stops accepting notifications or you delete your account.
  • Alert history (which alerts we sent you): kept while your account exists, deleted with it.
  • Crash reports (Sentry): 30 days.
  • Map telemetry (Mapbox): kept under Mapbox's own policy; Mapbox says it deletes IP addresses within 30 days and breaks the link to session identifiers within 24 hours. [ATTORNEY: confirm]
  • Hosting request logs (Render): 14 days; they can include IP addresses. Our own server logs contain no IP addresses or locations.
  • Database backups (Supabase): a daily backup is kept for 7 days, so something you delete can remain in a backup for up to 7 days. Photos and other uploaded files aren't part of these backups, so a deleted photo is gone right away. [ATTORNEY: confirm retention statement]

7. Deleting your account

Delete your account in the app (Profile → Settings). This removes your account, profile, profile photo, catch log and catch photos, saved streams and pins, alert settings and areas, follows, blocks, likes, notifications, push tokens, alert history, angler reports, reports you filed, feedback, and all the photos you uploaded. If you use Sign in with Apple we revoke the app's access, and we delete your record at RevenueCat. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel it in your Apple account settings.

So other people's conversations still make sense, the text of posts, comments and chat messages you wrote stays, shown as "Deleted user", with their photos removed. Delete any you don't want kept before deleting your account. If one of them is under review because someone reported it, it's kept, with its photo, until the review is done. Reports that other people filed about your content stay with our moderation records for 12 months.
