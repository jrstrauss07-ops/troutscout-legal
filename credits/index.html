TroutScout Credits

Last updated: October 23, 2026 · Home · Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

TroutScout is built on public data from government agencies, open map data and open-source software. None of these organizations sponsors or endorses TroutScout. Data is shown as published by its source and may be out of date or incomplete. Always check the official agency source before you fish.

Maps

Maps © Mapbox © OpenStreetMap. Satellite imagery © Maxar. Improve this map.

OpenStreetMap data is available under the Open Database License. A small number of trail names in TroutScout come from OpenStreetMap, through NH GRANIT and the Utah Geospatial Resource Center: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.

Weather and water

  • Weather: NOAA / National Weather Service forecasts and observations. This information is in the public domain and is not subject to copyright protection. Its use in TroutScout does not imply endorsement by NOAA or the National Weather Service.
  • Recent rain: NOAA Multi-Radar/Multi-Sensor System (MRMS) hourly precipitation estimates, from NOAA's National Centers for Environmental Prediction and the NOAA Open Data Dissemination program. This information is in the public domain. Its use in TroutScout does not imply endorsement by NOAA.
  • Rain gauge reports used to quality-check those estimates: NWS/FAA ASOS observations from the Iowa Environmental Mesonet, Iowa State University.
  • Stream flow and water temperature: Credit: U.S. Geological Survey. USGS data is in the U.S. public domain. Retrieved through the USGS Water Data APIs, using an api.data.gov key.

Fish Scores are TroutScout's own estimates. They are not forecasts from any of these sources.

Streams, lakes and land

  • Streams, lakes and watersheds: USGS National Hydrography Dataset (NHD and NHDPlus HR) and Watershed Boundary Dataset. The stream lines on the map are drawn from the NHD and served as map tiles through Mapbox.
  • County, state and tribal boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau, TIGERweb.
  • National Forests and Forest Service trails and campgrounds: USDA Forest Service. National Park units: National Park Service.
  • Wilderness areas: Wilderness Connect (University of Montana, with BLM, USFWS, USFS and NPS).
  • Pennsylvania: Pennsylvania Spatial Data Access (PASDA), with data from the PA Fish and Boat Commission, PA Game Commission, PA DCNR and PennDOT.
  • Other state data: Connecticut DEEP; Maine Conserved Lands; Maryland iMAP and DNR; New Hampshire GRANIT and NH Fish and Game; New York State DEC and data.ny.gov; North Carolina Wildlife Resources Commission; Tennessee Division of Forestry and TWRA; Utah Geospatial Resource Center; Virginia DWR and Department of Forestry; and other state fish, wildlife and natural-resource agencies.

Stocking and regulations

Trout stocking information comes from the public stocking reports of state fish and wildlife agencies. TroutScout's regulation pages are its own summaries of state regulations. They are not official copies. Check the agency's current regulations before you fish.

Artwork

TroutScout's illustrations, logos and backgrounds were made for TroutScout, many of them with AI image tools. The state artwork is TroutScout's own design and is not an official state emblem.
